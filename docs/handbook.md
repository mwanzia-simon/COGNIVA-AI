# Unlisted Code Logic company

Unlisted Code Logic is a technology company focused on developing innovative software solutions, digital products, and technology-driven services.

The company is led by its Chief Executive Officer (CEO), **Simon Makau**, who oversees its strategic direction, growth, and overall operations.

The company specializes in web development, mobile application development, software engineering, and IT consulting.

Its mission is to simplify everyday challenges through reliable, accessible, and practical technology solutions.

Unlisted Code Logic serves individuals, startups, small businesses, and organizations seeking digital transformation.

Its core products include custom business applications, e-commerce platforms, management systems, and web-based tools.

The company uses technologies such as Python, JavaScript, React, Node.js, C++, and MongoDB in its development projects.

Its development team follows structured software development practices, including planning, version control, testing, documentation, and maintenance.

The company values innovation, teamwork, continuous learning, professionalism, and customer satisfaction.

Unlisted Code Logic operates remotely, with team members collaborating through digital communication and project management tools.

Its security practices include access control, secure authentication, regular backups, and responsible handling of customer information.

The company's long-term vision is to become a recognized technology brand delivering scalable software products and services while creating opportunities for developers and businesses.
